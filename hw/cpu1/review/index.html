cpu1 — 100 × 80 mm, 4 layers

The digital control board: one STM32H743ZIT6 generating every hard real-time signal — PWM, PWM-synchronised sampling, the hardware trip — for a purely analog power board soldered underneath it. This is the board as it stands after M7a, placed in full and routed in part.

Read this before looking at copper. Routing is deliberately incomplete — board.mk says ROUTING := incomplete. The MCU core and the power block are routed; the safety chain, trip comparators, ADC networks and both field buses are placed only, and their 313 connections are left for M8, when the whole board is in view. DRC runs in the mode that still refuses anything drawn wrongly but does not demand what has not been drawn at all.

Renders

Top and bottom

KiCad's raytracer, from the same board file. No 3D model library is installed in this environment, so parts appear as their bare land patterns — these are useful for spacing, connector access and silkscreen legibility, and for nothing else. In particular a footprint renders identically whether or not its pinout is right.

Silkscreen still overlaps in the dense passive fields — the ADC network column on the left and the comparator outputs on the right. Known, tracked, and deferred with the rest of the finishing work to M8.
