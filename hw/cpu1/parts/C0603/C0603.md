## The 100 V part

`CC0603KRX7R0BB104`, [C113803](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C113803.html),
100 nF ±10 % X7R **100 V**, stock 1,504,477. The high-frequency capacitor at the
100 V buck's input pin, beside the two 2.2 µF in 1210.

Its rating is chosen the same way as theirs: against the TVS's breakdown
maximum, 51.1 V, because between the stand-off voltage and breakdown the TVS
conducts nothing and the input capacitors hold the rail alone. A 50 V 0603 —
the obvious part, and the one in every other position on this board — would sit
inside that window.
