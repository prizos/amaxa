component API on 2026-09-17. The same part led12 uses.

The Kangnex part drops into KiCad's Phoenix MKDS footprint exactly — same
5.08 mm pitch, same 1.30 mm drill — at a quarter of the price. **Do not
substitute the KF128**: its leads need a 1.60 mm drill.

9 to 36 V in, at the left edge where a person can reach it, turned so the wires
enter away from the board.
