Read from JLCPCB's component API on 2026-09-17.

The 10 µF is the same part led12 uses. Two of them make the 100 V buck's output
capacitance and one more the 3V3 converter's input, which its datasheet puts at
10 µF or more.

The 22 µF pair is the 3V3 rail's own output capacitance. Its value is not free:
the converter has no external compensation, and
`test_the_3v3_output_filter_is_the_one_the_datasheet_specifies` holds the whole
rail — these two and every decoupling capacitor on 3V3 — inside the 20 to 68 µF
the datasheet's Table 2 gives for a 3.3 V output.

X7R and not X5R for the 22 µF, because it is the one whose capacitance the
control loop depends on over temperature.

**Footprint.** KiCad stock `Capacitor_SMD:C_0805_2012Metric`, unmodified.
