## Why it is not an 0402 like everything else

A termination exists to be closed onto the bus. Behind an open jumper it
dissipates nothing, which is what a board as fabricated ships as — and for a
while that was the only case anyone had computed, so the part that could not
do its job passed every check by not doing it.

Closed, the THVD1450 drives up to 3.5 V differential into it. Across 120 Ω
that is 29 mA and **103 mW**, in a part rated 62.5. Not past this board's 50 %
derating: past the absolute maximum. The 1206's 250 mW leaves it at 41 % of
rating and 82 % of the derated limit, which is where a termination that is
meant to be fitted ought to sit.

`test_a_bus_termination_survives_the_fault_its_transceiver_declares` computes
both of those, and since the part can now meet them it asserts them instead of
printing them.

## And the voltage, which is a separate limit

The THVD1450 declares an 18 V bus fault voltage, and this board already used
that figure to check the connector's contacts. Across 120 Ω it is 2.7 W, which
no chip resistor of any size survives — a terminated node does not come
through a bus short with its termination intact, and that is normal. What the
check holds is the *standoff*: 18 V against this part's 200 V working voltage.

The 0402 that used to be here is rated 50 V, which clears 18 V but not the
**58 V** the CAN part declares next door. Power and voltage are different
questions about the same resistor, and only one of them was being asked.

## What it cost in layout

The termination chain stands on end between the two bus lanes, which are one
connector pitch apart, in a window bounded above by the transceiver's own A
leg crossing at y −39.365 and below by the connector's courtyard at −46.62.
Seven millimetres, against 1.65 + 2.28 mm of courtyard between a jumper's
centre and a 1206's, plus clearance at each end. It does not fit.

So JP2 left the column and stands beside the outer lane at (11.3, −41.0), the
way CAN's low-leg jumper already does, with RS485_TERM passing under the lane
on the back layer to reach the resistor. The 1206 has the column to itself.
