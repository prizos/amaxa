## Why 100 V for a 36 V rail

Two reasons, and neither is the rail voltage.

The LM5164's datasheet asks for a voltage rating of **twice the maximum input**
on a ceramic, because capacitance falls with DC bias: an X7R at its rating
keeps a fraction of its marked value, and the 2.2 µF the converter needs is a
2.2 µF it can actually see.

And `test_every_capacitor_on_the_input_survives_an_unclamped_overvoltage`
compares against the TVS's **breakdown** maximum, 51.1 V, not its stand-off:
between those two voltages the TVS conducts nothing and these capacitors hold
the rail by themselves.

Two of them, plus a 100 nF in 0603, at the converter's input pin.
