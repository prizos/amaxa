All are 1 %, 125 mW, 150 V, ±100 ppm/°C, 0805.

**Pin mapping.** Symmetrical two-terminal part; pad 1 and pad 2 are interchangeable.

**Footprint.** KiCad stock `Resistor_SMD:R_0805_2012Metric`, unmodified.

**Trade-off recorded.** All four are JLCPCB *Extended* parts, which costs extra
on assembly setup. The equivalent UNI-ROYAL set is entirely *Basic* and cheaper
(0805W8F6800T5E / C17798, 0805W8F1002T5E / C17414, 0805W8F1003T5E / C149504,
0805W8F3301T5E / C26010), at the price of four supplier lines instead of one.
Worth revisiting if this board is ever assembled in quantity.
