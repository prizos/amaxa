# LED0805 — 0805 red indicator LED

| | |
|---|---|
| Component | `LED_RED` |
| Manufacturer | Everlight |
| Part number | `17-21SURC/S530-A3/4T` |
| LCSC | [C2943978](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C2943978.html) |
| Footprint | `LED_0805_2012Metric` |

Red, 624–632 nm, 60 mcd, **Vf 2.0–2.4 V at 20 mA**. Stock 196,650, $0.0147 at
qty 100. Extended.

**Why this part.** Chosen for the *bounded* forward voltage. The obvious cheap
alternative, NationStar NCD0805R1 (C84256, Basic, $0.0135), is specified
1.6–2.6 V at 25 mA — a 1 V spread on a 12 V rail through 680 Ω swings the branch
current by about 1.5 mA, and more importantly that figure is LCSC parametric
data that could not be confirmed against the datasheet, whose tables are images.
A 0.4 V spread that is written down in the datasheet is worth $0.0012 a part.

**Pin mapping.** The KiCad land pattern's pad 1 is the cathode: it is the end
the silkscreen bar sits at, and `Device:LED`'s pin 1 is K. The netlist puts
`LED_RETURN` — the net that runs to the low-side FET's drain — on pad 1, which
is the cathode side, and that is right.

**Everlight numbers their own terminals the other way round.** Their package
drawing, `evidence/polarity.png`, marks terminal **2** as the cathode with the
minus sign and the diode's bar, terminal 1 as the anode, and puts the cathode
mark at the terminal-2 end. So "pad 1 is the cathode" and "terminal 1 is the
anode" are both true and are about different numbering: the land pattern's and
the manufacturer's. Anyone comparing the two straight across will conclude the
board is backwards, and it is not.

Nothing physical turns on either number. **A chip LED is oriented by its
polarity mark against the board's**, and
`test_every_polarised_part_marks_its_cathode_on_the_silkscreen` asserts the
silkscreen bar is at the end the netlist calls the cathode. What was missing
was confirmation that this part *has* a cathode mark and where it is, and that
is what the drawing settles.

**This used to say "needs a human eye", and the reason is worth keeping.** The
datasheet LCSC serves for C2943978 is a NATIONSTAR specification behind an
EVERLIGHT part number, so it could never settle this part's polarity, and
Everlight's published path returned 404. The document above is Everlight
Europe's own, DSE-0025713 Rev.2, fetched with its sha256 recorded in
`evidence/sources.json`. The lesson stands: a distributor's PDF link is not
evidence about the part it is filed under.

**Footprint.** KiCad stock `LED_SMD:LED_0805_2012Metric`, unmodified.
