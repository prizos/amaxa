468 series, 1 A, 63 V, Slo-Blo. I²t 0.127 A²s, 50 A breaking capacity. Stock
18,500, $0.0758 at qty 100. Extended.

**Corrected.** First written down as 0466001.NR, chosen for slow-blow behaviour.
The **466 series is fast-acting** — the datasheet title says so — and that exact
variant has zero stock besides. The 468 is the Slo-Blo part, with three times
the I²t, which is what lets it survive the inrush into the bulk capacitor
without nuisance-blowing.

Also checked and rejected: 437 series (1206, fast-acting), 467 series (0603, not
1206), JFC1206FS (fast-acting).

**Headroom.** Worst-case board draw is roughly 60 mA of LED current plus the
regulator, so the 1 A rating is about 15× the load. It is there for faults, not
for overload discrimination.

**Pin mapping.** Symmetrical two-terminal part.

**Footprint.** KiCad stock `Fuse:Fuse_1206_3216Metric`, unmodified.
