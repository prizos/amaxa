# SOT223 — SOT-223 linear regulator

| | |
|---|---|
| Component | ``Ldo3v3`` |
| Manufacturer | Advanced Monolithic Systems |
| Part number | `AMS1117-3.3` |
| LCSC | [C6186](https://www.lcsc.com/product-detail/C6186.html) |

1 A adjustable-family fixed 3.3 V LDO. Stock 955,570, $0.1737 at qty 100. Basic.

**Pin mapping.** Pin 1 = GND/ADJ, pin 2 = VOUT, pin 3 = VIN, tab = VOUT. Read
from the AMS datasheet text, and it matches the KiCad `SOT-223-3_TabPin2`
footprint, whose pin 2 is the tab.

**Footprint.** KiCad stock `Package_TO_SOT_SMD:SOT-223-3_TabPin2`, unmodified.

**Open issue: this part is being replaced.** Its absolute maximum input is 15 V,
while the input TVS clamps at 19.9 V. During a surge the regulator, not the TVS,
is the weakest part on the rail — the protection lets the thing it protects take
the hit. A 12 V stand-off TVS cannot clamp below 15 V, so the regulator has to
move, not the TVS. A replacement rated above about 24 V is being sourced.

**Thermal, for whatever replaces it.** At 12 V in and 3.3 V out the regulator
drops 8.7 V. The only load here is a 3.3 kΩ resistor, about 1 mA, so dissipation
is dominated by quiescent current; but at 100 mA it would be 0.87 W in a
SOT-223, which is more than the package wants.

**Do not substitute** the cheaper UMW AMS1117-3.3 (C347222): it is listed at a
12 V maximum input, which this rail is at nominally and exceeds at 13.2 V.
