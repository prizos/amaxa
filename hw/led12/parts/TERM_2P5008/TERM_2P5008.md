# TERM_2P5008 — 2-pole 5.08 mm screw terminal

2 poles, 5.08 mm pitch, 18 A / 250 V, M2.5 screws, AWG 14–30, through hole.
Stock 268,025, $0.1087 at qty 100. Extended.

**Why this part for a Phoenix footprint.** The footprint is KiCad's Phoenix MKDS
outline and the genuine Phoenix 1715721 does exist on LCSC (C480516, $0.4261),
but the Kangnex part drops into the same holes at a quarter of the price: pads
at ±2.540 mm, exactly 5.08 mm pitch, with a **1.30 mm drill** identical to the
footprint's.

**Do not substitute KF128-5.08-2P** (C474952), the popular cheap clone: its
leads need a **1.60 mm** drill and will not fit these 1.3 mm holes.

**Body fit.** KiCad's fabrication outline is 10.16 × 9.80 mm; the Kangnex body
is about 10.76 × 10.16 mm, so it overhangs by roughly 0.3 mm per side — within
the footprint's own silkscreen to about 0.2 mm. Electrically irrelevant, but the
courtyard is tight if something is placed hard against it. Worth a look during
placement review.

**Pin mapping.** Pin 1 and pin 2 are the two poles. Pin 1 carries the 12 V
input, pin 2 the return.

**Footprint.** KiCad stock, unmodified. Body dimensions above came from the
verified EasyEDA footprint geometry, not the manufacturer drawing, which is an
image.
